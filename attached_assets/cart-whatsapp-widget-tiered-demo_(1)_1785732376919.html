Demo controls (simulate real behavior)

Add a product to begin. In a real store, Tier 1 waits ~45–60s of idle time on its own — here, tap "fast-forward" to skip the wait.

Real logic being tested: Tier 1 fires once, quietly, after you've been idle a bit post add-to-cart. If you dismiss it, it goes silent. Tier 2 only fires if you then show a real leave-signal (switching tabs/apps, or trying to close) — and only once. Convert or dismiss twice, and it stops for the session. The hook text also changes once you have 2+ items in cart.
